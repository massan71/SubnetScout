## What's new: continuous monitoring

- **Scheduled scans.**
  - In Settings (⌘,) › Schedule choose the frequency (hourly, every 6 hours, daily, weekly), profile and network. By default the network is the current one, detected automatically.
  - Scans go through the helper and never ask for the password.
  - If the Mac was off or asleep at the scheduled time, the scan starts as soon as possible.
- **Notifications only when something changes**: new devices not marked as known, opened ports, new issues of medium severity or higher, new CVEs.
  - Notifications contain only counts, never addresses or names, because they can appear on the lock screen.
  - A click opens the scan.
  - "Send Test Notification" checks that notifications are allowed.
- **Menu bar icon** with the last scan, grade, next scan and "Scan Now". With the icon on, the app stays open even when you close the window, so the schedule keeps running.
- **Open at login**, to keep scheduled scans running after a restart.
- During a scan macOS does not pause the app (App Nap) and the Mac does not go to sleep.

## Good to know

- The first scheduled scan is the baseline and triggers no notifications.
- With the automatic target, networks larger than /22 (over 1,024 addresses) are skipped.

## After updating

The helper has changed: click **Reinstall** in the sidebar and approve it again in System Settings › General › Login Items & Extensions.
