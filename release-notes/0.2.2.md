## What's new

- **Every scan goes through the helper** when it is installed, even without "As administrator". macOS no longer limits them (before, a Discovery without administrator could find less than half of the devices) and a password is never needed. "As administrator" only decides whether to add OS detection, UDP and the DHCP probe.
- **Reliable cancellation in password mode.** Without the helper, stopping a scan now really stops the root nmap within a second, even if the app quits or crashes. Before, nmap kept running on its own in the background. This mode also has a progress bar now.
- **The helper only scans private networks**: 10/8, 172.16/12, 192.168/16, link-local, CGNAT, loopback, and for IPv6 fe80::/10, fc00::/7 and ::1.
  - Hostnames are resolved and verified before the scan.
  - Scans of public addresses run with nmap as a normal user.
