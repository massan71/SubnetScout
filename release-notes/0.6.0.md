## SubnetScout is now in English

The whole app has been translated to English:

- the interface: sidebar, results, device details, Network view, Settings, menu bar and setup assistant;
- every audit finding, with its description and remediation;
- the HTML report and CSV export;
- notifications, error messages (including those from the privileged helper) and the macOS permission prompts;
- the README, the release notes and the Homebrew cask.

Scans saved by earlier versions keep their original Italian text for the findings recorded at the time.

## After updating

- **Keychain**: choose “Always Allow” when macOS asks for access to the scan history.
- **Helper**: click **Reinstall** in the sidebar and approve it again.

macOS will ask for both after every update, because the app is not signed with an Apple Developer ID.
