## What's new: network audit

- **ARP spoofing**: flags when the gateway MAC changes compared with the previous scan, and when another device answers with the gateway's MAC.
- **Rogue DHCP servers**: as administrator, with the Standard and Full profiles, SubnetScout collects the DHCP offers on the network (`broadcast-dhcp-discover`). It flags those coming from servers other than the expected one.
- **Unusual DNS**: flags a DNS assigned by another device on the LAN (normal with Pi-hole or AdGuard) and an unrecognized external DNS.
- **Ports open to the Internet (UPnP)**: finds routers with UPnP enabled and reads, read-only, their port forwarding table.
  - Every forwarded port is reported and linked to the device that receives it.
  - A router with UPnP enabled is reported even if it has no open ports at the moment.
- **Router management**: flags SSH, Telnet or SNMP reachable on the router.
- **New service checks**:
  - HTTPS without HSTS;
  - risky HTTP methods (PUT, DELETE, TRACE);
  - weak Diffie-Hellman parameters;
  - DSA SSH host keys, or RSA below 2048 bits.
- **"Network" view** in the app with gateway, DHCP, DNS and UPnP routers. The HTML report also has a new Network section.

## Good to know

- The UPnP check and reading MACs without privileges require the **Local Network** permission (System Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network). If the permission is missing, the app tells you.
- The scripts run as administrator have changed: after updating, click **Reinstall** in the sidebar and approve the helper again.
- Scans saved with 0.1.0 can still be opened, but they have no data for the Network view.
